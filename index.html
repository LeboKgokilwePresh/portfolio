About

Support that keeps things running, not just tickets closed

I'm a Network Administrator and IT Support Engineer based in Midrand, Gauteng, supporting enterprise IT infrastructure across hospitality and managed-support environments — where downtime has an immediate, visible cost, so I've learned to diagnose fast and communicate clearly while I do it.

I'm comfortable across the stack: switch port administration on Cisco infrastructure, TCP/IP, DNS, DHCP, VLANs and VPN connectivity, administering Active Directory accounts and security groups, and resolving Level 2/3 incidents within SLA under ITIL process. I've also worked directly with vendors on upgrades and infrastructure maintenance, and installed network devices, printers and wireless access points on-site.
